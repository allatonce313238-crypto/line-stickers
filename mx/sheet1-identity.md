## Prompt

```
Using the attached images (three stickers of the same character) as the exact character reference, create ONE character design reference sheet, landscape 3:2, plain light cream background, the same 16-bit pixel art style with bold dark outlines and bright saturated colors, cute chibi proportions. Ignore the text, speech bubbles, white sticker borders and effects in the stickers; use only the character. Keep the design IDENTICAL everywhere on the sheet.

Character: Mx, a small chibi knight CAT.
- Face: round cat face, big round BLUE eyes with white shine (a blue gradient, dark navy at the top to light sky blue at the bottom), small pink nose, small cat mouth with tiny fangs, rosy cheeks.
- Fur: light brown tabby with darker brown stripes, cream muzzle and chest, pink inner ears.
- Helmet: silver-and-blue knight helmet with a gold cross badge on the front, gold rim, a blue gem on each side, and two small white feathered wings on the sides.
- Armor: silver armor with gold trim and blue panels, a blue tabard with a gold cross, brown leather belt with a gold buckle, silver arm and leg armor.
- Cape: royal blue cape with small gold stars and a gold edge.
- Shield: blue kite shield with a silver rim and a large gold star/snowflake emblem with a blue gem in the center.
- Tail: fluffy, striped brown and cream (ringed, like a raccoon tail).
- Paws: cream paws with pink pads.

Layout (same as a game character sheet):
Header: title "MX", subtitle "CHARACTER IDENTITY SHEET".
Row 1, TURNAROUND: full body, same size and ground line: Front, 3/4 Front, Back, Side.
Row 2, EXPRESSIONS: six head-and-shoulder close-ups: Neutral, Happy (eyes closed ^_^), Excited, Angry, Surprised, Sleepy. No tears anywhere.
Row 3 left, COLOR PALETTE: 8 labeled swatches: Fur Brown, Stripe Dark Brown, Cream, Armor Silver, Royal Blue, Gold, Pink, Eye Blue #3B9FF5.
Row 3 right, SEPARATED PARTS: head with helmet, helmet wings, body with armor, arms, legs, cape, shield, tail.

Text: only the header and small plain labels. No other text, no speech bubbles, no tears, no scenery, no ground shadow.
```
