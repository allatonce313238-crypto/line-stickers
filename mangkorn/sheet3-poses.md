## Prompt

```
Open this image, the identity sheet of the character Mangkorn the chibi red dragon: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allatonce313238-crypto/line-stickers/claude/line-stickers-bg-dicut-302vim/mangkorn/references/sheet1-identity-final.png
Create ONE new pose reference sheet in exactly the same style and layout look: landscape 3:2, 16-bit pixel art with bold dark outlines, cute chibi proportions, same header style with the title "MANGKORN" and the subtitle "STICKER POSE SHEET". The dragon must look IDENTICAL to the reference: same face, amber eyes, horns with gold bands, gold crown with red gem, red scales with gold diamond studs, cream fluffy fur, dark brown hand paws, pink-padded feet, red wings with gold edges, fluffy red-and-cream tail.

Do NOT reuse poses like idle, walk, jump, fly, attack, defend. Draw EXACTLY these 9 poses, 3 columns x 3 rows, each in its own panel with its number and name label. Full body, 3/4 front view (body turned halfway, face toward the viewer):
1. WAVE: standing, waving one paw high above his head, happy smile.
2. SIGN: standing, holding a BLANK round white sign above his head with both paws. Nothing written or drawn on the sign.
3. BOW: bowing forward politely, one paw on his chest, wings folded, eyes closed.
4. PLEASE: both paws pressed together under his chin, pleading, big shiny eyes.
5. LOVE: both paws making a heart shape in front of his chest, happy smile.
6. LOL: sitting on the ground, laughing hard with eyes squeezed shut, pointing one claw forward at the viewer.
7. SURPRISED: jumping back, both paws up near his cheeks, mouth open, eyes wide.
8. TIRED: lying flat on his belly, completely exhausted, arms and wings spread out limp, half-closed eyes.
9. HUG: standing, hugging a small round plush doll with both arms, happy closed eyes. The doll is a simple round blob with dot eyes and no mouth.

No tears on any pose. No speech bubbles, no extra words: only the header and the panel labels. Small effects (sparkles, motion lines, small hearts) are OK.
```
