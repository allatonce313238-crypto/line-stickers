# Mangkorn: Sheet 1, Identity (turnaround, expressions, palette, parts)

Purpose: lock Mangkorn's look. This is a reference sheet, not animation frames.

Attach as reference: the Quest poster and the three dragon stickers (Fighting!, Hungry, zZZ).

## Prompt

```
Using the attached images as the exact character reference, create ONE character design reference sheet, landscape 3:2, plain flat white background, 16-bit pixel art style with bold dark outlines and bright saturated colors, cute chibi proportions. Keep the design IDENTICAL everywhere on the sheet.

Character: a small chibi red dragon with a cat-like face.
- Face: cat-like, big round amber/orange eyes with a white highlight, small pink nose, small fangs, rosy blush cheeks.
- Head: fluffy cream/white fur tufts on the ears and cheeks; two dark brown horns with gold bands; a gold crown/tiara with a red gem on the forehead.
- Body: red scales with gold diamond-shaped studs, a cream/white fluffy belly and chest, gold bracelets on the arms, small paws with pink pads and dark claws.
- Wings: large red wings with gold-edged membrane, bat-like with a warm orange inner membrane.
- Tail: thick red scaled tail, gold diamond studs, cream tuft at the tip.

Layout:
Top row: full-body turnaround, same size and same ground line: front, 3/4 front, side, back.
Middle row: six head close-ups on the same grid: neutral, happy (eyes closed ^_^), sad (teary), angry, surprised, sleepy.
Bottom row: left, a color palette strip with 8 labeled color swatches; right, a "parts breakdown" showing the separated parts, each drawn clean on its own: head with horns and crown, torso, left arm, right arm, left wing, right wing, tail.

Text: only small labels in plain English (Front, 3/4, Side, Back, Neutral, Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprised, Sleepy, Palette, Parts). No other text, no logos. No scenery, no ground shadow.
```
